How It Works

From your photo & details to a sparkly gold-glitter cake topper — five simple steps.

1

Send Your Photos & Details

Share your photo(s), the name and age (or text), event date, theme or colors, and whether you need matching cupcake picks. Tell us your preferred size and style.

2

Photoshop Edit + Digital Proof

We carefully edit your photo in Photoshop — adjusting color, cropping, and composing the design with gold glitter lettering. You'll receive a digital proof within 24 hours.

3

Approve Your Design

Review the proof and request any tweaks. Once you approve, we move to production. One round of minor revisions is included.

4

Print & Cricut-Cut

Your design is printed on thick glossy glitter cardstock, then precision machine-cut with a Cricut for clean, professional edges. Toppers are decorative and not edible — remove before cutting and serving.

5

Ship or Local Pickup

We carefully package your topper for shipping, or arrange free local pickup in American Fork & Utah County. You'll get tracking info as soon as it ships.

Turnaround & Rush Options

Standard turnaround is 5–7 business days after proof approval (plus shipping). Need it sooner? Rush production (+$10, 2–3 business days) is available as an add-on — just note your party date when ordering so we can plan ahead.
